# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Evict welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and
experience level. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, and
assume good intent while remaining precise about technical concerns.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publishing private information, or sustained personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will avoid
conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
